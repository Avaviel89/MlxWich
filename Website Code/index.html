About MIxWich

Founded in 2020 as a small street-side shop in Jakarta, MIxWich has grown into Indonesia's premier customizable sandwich brand. Our commitment to fresh, premium ingredients and customer satisfaction has driven our expansion to three locations across Jakarta, Surabaya, and Denpasar.

At MIxWich, we believe every customer deserves their perfect sandwich. That's why we offer complete customization - from artisan-baked bread to high-quality sliced meats, organic vegetables, and our signature homemade sauces. Every ingredient is carefully selected to ensure the highest quality and freshness.
